### LaTeX template for course notes

*LaTeX, updated November 2026*

A clean layout for typesetting lecture notes, with theorem and definition boxes, a title block for course and term, and wide margins for annotations. Compiles with pdfLaTeX or LuaLaTeX.

- [course-notes.tex](/assets/templates/course-notes.tex) (TEX)
- [Example output](/assets/templates/course-notes-example.pdf) (PDF)
- [Open in Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/)
